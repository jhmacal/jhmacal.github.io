Services

I provide document, writing, and language services on a fixed fee basis. I quote every engagement individually before any work begins. I do not use retainers, subscriptions, or recurring billing.

What I offer

Consultation and strategy session, 60 minutes. A one hour working session to understand your goals, review your situation, discuss the options available to you, and outline practical next steps for your business or documentation needs. $500 for the session. Document preparation and any follow up work is quoted separately.

Business and professional plans. Written plans prepared to your specifications, covering the structure, operations, market position, and financial projections you need to present. I quote these by scope and complexity.

Certified translation into English. Translation of your documents into English, delivered with a signed certificate of accuracy. $0.75 per page. I negotiate package rates for larger volumes.

Document review and preparation. Drafting, review, editing, and formatting of documents you need to produce. I quote these by scope and complexity.

How it works

  1. You describe what you need. I send a written quote setting out the scope, the fee, and the delivery date.
  2. You accept the quote in writing.
  3. You pay the invoice by card or bank transfer.
  4. I deliver the completed work.

Delivery

Standard delivery is one week from acceptance of the quote. If a project requires longer, the timeline appears in the quote before you accept it.

What you receive

The deliverable is the completed document, translation, review, or session itself. I do not promise, guarantee, or control any outcome, approval, or decision made by a third party who receives the work.

Revisions

One round of revisions is included with every written deliverable. Corrections of material errors on my side do not count against that round.

Cancellation and refunds

Acceptance of a quote is binding. If you cancel after acceptance, I will charge proportionally for the work already performed. Once I deliver the completed work, the fee is earned in full.

Contact

jh@jhmacal.com. I respond to questions, revision requests, and problems with delivered work within one business day.

Scope of services

These are document, writing, and language services. They do not include legal advice or legal representation, and no attorney client relationship arises from any engagement described on this page.
