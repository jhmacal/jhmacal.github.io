Professional profile

Legal engineering, knowledge management and AI governance

I am a Senior Attorney and Legal AI Operator admitted in New York and Brazil, with 15+ years of legal experience spanning practice and legal innovation. My practice experience started with litigation and transactions, and currently focuses on regulatory and corporate matters. Over the years, I have worked in legal engineering, knowledge management and artificial intelligence (AI) governance. I have led legal teams for the past decade, an 8-person multi-tier team in Brazil and a 5-person multi-tier legal team across 150 concurrent matters in the U.S.

Alongside the practice, I build the systems legal work runs on: workflows taken apart by the kind of work each step performs, AI tools grounded in sources a lawyer can open, and controls that keep release with the responsible attorney. My articles and case studies apply that method to conflicts intake, knowledge management, prompt engineering and AI governance.

Expertise

Legal engineering and workflow design

I map legal work across actors, inputs, handoffs and decisions, then separate drafting, source-backed retrieval, fixed-rule configuration and attorney judgment. The map becomes a scoped use case, technical requirements, controls and tests. I build legal AI systems with OpenAI and Anthropic application programming interfaces (APIs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), which connects model output to retrieved source material, Model Context Protocol (MCP), which connects AI tools to data and services, Python, JavaScript, n8n, Claude Code and Codex.

Knowledge management and attorney resources

I convert recurring attorney reasoning into model documents, legal memoranda, playbooks, decision rules and standard operating procedures, and connect those assets to their sources, intended use and review history. My work includes memoranda on novel legal theories that partners and associates adopted as practice templates, internal-resource retrieval and AI workflows grounded in the documents lawyers already use.

AI governance, implementation and adoption

I design controls for data handling, risk classification, vendor review, testing, human oversight and escalation, mapping governance documentation to the National Institute of Standards and Technology AI Risk Management Framework (NIST AI RMF), ISO/IEC 42001, the international standard for AI management systems, the European Union AI Act and American Bar Association (ABA) professional-responsibility requirements, including Formal Opinion 512. I carry adoption through workflow integration, training and feedback; my experience includes a 2021 practice-wide platform rollout and a compliance-screening pilot that reached first value in under 8 weeks and utilization above 80% at 6 months.
