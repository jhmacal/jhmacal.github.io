Governance example

The approval becomes a visible path

The meeting scene lets the learner watch the condition set assemble. The point is not that the committee says yes. The point is what the yes attaches to.

  • The use case narrows the permission.
  • The controls are part of the permission.
  • The owner keeps the process alive after approval.
